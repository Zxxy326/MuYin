GeoNames 国内城市表
====================
City data derived from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/).
Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The bundled table (geonames_cn.json) contains Chinese populated places with
coordinates, population and admin1 code, used only to resolve the nearest city
for weather display. Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com/).
